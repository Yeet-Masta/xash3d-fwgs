Xash3D FWGS Web

Half-Life and Counter-Strike 1.6 in your browser, powered by an open-source GoldSrc-compatible engine.

Bring your own game files

No game content is included here. You need a legally owned copy of Half-Life (and Counter-Strike for CS 1.6), for example from Steam.

  1. In Steam, right-click Half-Life → Manage → Browse local files.
  2. Put the valve folder (plus cstrike for Counter-Strike) into one .zip.
  3. Drop the zip below and press Play.

Your files stay on this device: they are read locally and never uploaded.
